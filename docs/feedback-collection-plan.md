# Feedback Collection Plan

## Purpose

This plan defines how MindMap Care should collect, review, and act on feedback from users, patients or clients, clinicians, supervisors, and programme leaders during pilot preparation and controlled pilot use.

## Feedback Groups

| Group | Feedback focus |
| --- | --- |
| Screeners | form clarity, consent workflow, time burden, missing-data friction |
| Clinician reviewers | explanation usefulness, risk category fit, override reasons, referral practicality |
| Supervisors | queue visibility, follow-up gaps, data completeness, support burden |
| Programme leads | operational value, reporting needs, staffing, scale readiness |
| Patients or clients | clarity, comfort, privacy, burden, trust where local approval allows |
| Technical support | downtime, errors, access issues, deployment and log gaps |

## Collection Methods

- short post-walkthrough feedback form
- weekly pilot check-in notes
- structured clinician case review
- issue and incident log
- training completion feedback
- supervisor monitoring review
- end-of-pilot debrief

## Minimum Feedback Fields

- date
- site
- role
- workflow stage
- feedback type
- severity or priority
- free-text observation
- suggested change
- owner
- status
- decision

## Feedback Types

- usability
- workflow fit
- explanation clarity
- safety concern
- privacy concern
- data quality
- referral or follow-up gap
- technical issue
- training need
- reporting need

## Review Cadence

| Cadence | Review focus |
| --- | --- |
| Daily during first week | urgent safety, privacy, downtime, and blocker feedback |
| Weekly during pilot | workflow friction, referral gaps, explanation issues, training needs |
| Monthly or end-of-pilot | product direction, validation findings, go/no-go recommendation |
| After any incident | containment, root cause, mitigation, pause decision |

## Triage Rules

| Severity | Meaning | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Critical | possible patient harm, crisis mishandling, privacy breach, unsafe use | immediate escalation and pause decision |
| High | could change triage action or expose sensitive data | assign owner and review before continued expansion |
| Medium | repeated workflow or usability issue | prioritize for next release or training update |
| Low | minor wording, layout, or documentation issue | batch into backlog |

## Patient Or Client Feedback

Patient or client feedback should be collected only if approved by local governance. If approved, keep it brief and avoid collecting sensitive unnecessary narrative.

Example topics:

- whether screening purpose was clear
- whether privacy felt protected
- whether the process was too long or confusing
- whether next steps were understandable

## Feedback Outputs

The pilot team should maintain:

- weekly feedback summary
- critical or high-risk issue log
- product change decisions
- training updates
- unresolved questions
- end-of-pilot learning summary

## Closeout Questions

At pilot closeout, reviewers should answer:

- Did the workflow help prioritize review and follow-up?
- Were explanations understandable and action-oriented?
- Did staff feel the tool fit their work?
- Did any risk category or recommendation feel unsafe?
- What operational burden did the tool add or reduce?
- What must change before expanding to another site?
